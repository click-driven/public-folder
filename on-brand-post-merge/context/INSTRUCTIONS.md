# Strict Text Preservation Rule

When merging the post content from the exported google doc with the sample-post layout template, you must adhere strictly to the following constraints:

1. **Zero Text Alterations:** Do NOT rephrase, reword, edit, optimize, or adjust the original wording from the exported google doc content under any circumstances.
2. **No Additions or Deletions:** Do NOT insert new commentary, preamble, summary notes, or explanations. Do NOT omit, truncate, or drop any original words, sentences, or punctuation.
